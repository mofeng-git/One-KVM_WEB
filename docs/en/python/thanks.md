### Paid Sponsorship Plan

To keep the project healthy and help cover device and service costs, we are piloting a paid sponsorship plan.

**What you get as a sponsor:**

> Note: Integrated images do not have performance or feature differences compared to public images, and we do not guarantee that every image offers a great experience. Actual performance depends on the hardware.

1. Public acknowledgement (docs/GitHub)

2. Access to all paid images (JetKVM integrated image and future One-KVM integrated images for new devices)

3. Priority technical support

4. Free NAT traversal service (One-KVM only)

**To become a sponsor:**

1. Sponsorship of 20 RMB or more

**Other:**

We accept hardware donations (not currently accepting hardware that must be returned after adaptation).

Cloud service providers (CDN, cloud servers, etc.) are also welcome to sponsor via community ads and website acknowledgements. If interested, contact the author via QQ 3463289142 (please include your purpose).
